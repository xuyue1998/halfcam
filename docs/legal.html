购买

Purchases

HALF CAM 通过 Apple 内购提供年卡、买断与加急券。价格以购买时 App Store 显示为准。

HALF CAM offers a yearly plan, a lifetime unlock, and rush tickets through Apple In-App Purchase. Prices are those shown in the App Store at the time of purchase.

年卡。自动续订，期限一年。确认购买后，费用从您的 Apple ID 账户扣除，并在当前周期结束前 24 小时内自动续订，除非您提前关闭自动续订。管理或取消：iPhone 设置 → Apple ID → 订阅。未使用期间不提供退款，除非适用法律另有要求。

Yearly plan. Auto-renewing, one year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID on confirmation, and renews within 24 hours before the current period ends unless you turn off auto-renew in advance. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Unused time is not refunded except where required by law.

买断。一次性非消耗型购买，无限装卷。等待不变。可在同一 Apple ID 下恢复。

Lifetime. A one-time non-consumable purchase. Unlimited rolls. Waiting times do not change. Restorable on the same Apple ID.

加急券。一张结束一卷的显影等待。每月赠送：免费 1 张、年卡 3 张、买断 6 张，过月重置。另可购买消耗型加急包（3 张），价格以购买时 App Store 显示为准。买的未用余数随 iCloud 钥匙值存储同步到同一 Apple ID；重装应用后可找回未用张数。未开启 iCloud 时，删除 App 后余量清除；系统设置中的“卸载 App”会保留数据。退款或交易撤销时，只扣买的余数（扣到 0 为止）。已用的不补发。

Rush tickets. One ticket ends one roll’s wait. Monthly grants: 1 on Free, 3 on Yearly, 6 on Lifetime, resetting each month. A consumable pack of 3 tickets may also be purchased at the App Store price. Unused purchased tickets sync via iCloud Key-Value Store to the same Apple ID and can be restored after reinstalling. If iCloud is off, the balance is lost when the app is deleted; Offload App in Settings keeps app data. A refund or revoked transaction claws back unused purchased tickets down to zero. Used tickets are not reissued.
